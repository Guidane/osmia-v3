🏷 Access tags

A record, a page or a space with access tags is seen only by people who hold every one of them; everyone else doesn't see it at all, not even that it's there. Tags are not ranks: holding one gives nothing in any other, administrators included. A record also carries the tags of its parent in a tree and of the spaces it links to. Only a tag's owners give it to people; the other owners are told each time.
