◆ Extensions

Apps are made on the website. An extension app is made in code, with screens of its own that stay as they are, like Documentation or the AI prompt generator. It keeps its data in ordinary tables, and can add blocks, tabs, panels, fields, view types and automation actions to every app. Switching one on lists what it needs and installs it; switching one off removes only its code: its data stays, as ordinary apps and fields. Either takes effect when Osmia restarts.
