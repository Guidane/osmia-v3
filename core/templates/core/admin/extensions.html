◆ Extensions

Apps are made on the website. When an app needs more than tables, views and automations, such as a drawing canvas, that part is an extension: code in the extensions/ folder. Extensions can add view types, field types, automation actions, boxes on record pages and pages of their own. Switching one on or off takes effect when Osmia restarts.
